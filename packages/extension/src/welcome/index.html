    2. Try it
    
        
    
          
    With Chrome open, ask your agent:
    
          
    Organise my open tabs into groups by topic. Show me the plan first.
    
          
    Open the Chrome release notes and summarise what changed in the latest version.
    
          
    The status above turns green once your agent's server is running. Changes made with chrome_plan_apply can be reverted by asking the agent to undo.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    With Safari open, ask your agent:

    Put my Safari tabs in order by site.
    
          
    
            The status above turns green once your agent's server is running. In Safari your agent can open, arrange and close windows, and open,
            navigate, switch, pin, mute, duplicate, move and close tabs. If tab titles and addresses are missing, give the extension website access in Safari under Settings > Extensions.
            Tab groups, history, bookmarks and page control are available in Chrome.
          
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Privacy
    
        
    
          Your browsing data stays on your computer: the extension only talks to 127.0.0.1. Your agent can read tab titles and URLs,
          rearrange, group or close tabs, and read and act in web pages when you ask it to. Chrome shows a debugging banner while it
          works in a page. Tab titles, URLs and page contents are never sent anywhere else by this extension.

    Privacy
    
        
        
    
          
    
            Your browsing data stays on your computer: the extension only talks to 127.0.0.1. Your agent can read tab titles and URLs,
            rearrange, group or close tabs, and read and act in web pages when you ask it to. Chrome shows a debugging banner while it works in a page.
            Tab titles, URLs and page contents are never sent anywhere else by this extension.
